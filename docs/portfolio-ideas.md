# Portfolio Ideas Vault

These directions are intentionally not part of the first build. Keep them as
possible future branches once the guided archive is established.

## Rooms

An immersive portfolio made of distinct, dark spaces. Experience, the AI
Challenge award, and hackathon work each become a separate room with its own
camera-like entrance and exit.

Why revisit it:
- Strongest cinematic payoff for individual milestones.
- Could suit a later version with richer project material or imagery.

Trade-off:
- Navigation is more demanding and can become heavy on mobile.

## Film Strip

A horizontal sequence of full-screen scenes that snap into place as a visitor
scrolls. The story reads as a paced reel rather than a continuous archive.

Why revisit it:
- Highly memorable and well suited to a focused career narrative.
- Makes milestones feel intentional and editorial.

Trade-off:
- Less natural than vertical scrolling, especially for mobile visitors and
  accessibility preferences.

## Portrait Layer

Introduce a considered portrait of Spyros later, once there is an image that
belongs with the rest of the visual language. It could appear as a quiet
interlude in the archive rather than as a conventional hero headshot.

Why revisit it:
- Adds a personal anchor without weakening the abstract first release.

## Light Mode

Consider a light interpretation only after the dark archive has settled. It
should be a deliberate inverse composition with black type and burnt-orange
accents, not an automatic color swap.

Why revisit it:
- Gives visitors an optional reading mode while preserving the dark-first
  identity.
